Meet your child's doctor

Dr. Prashant Patil, बालरोग तज्ञ.

Dr. Prashant Patil is a paediatrician with an M.D. in Paediatrics who leads Yugveer Child Clinic in Moshi. His work starts as early as the first minutes of life and continues through every stage of childhood.

Parents come to him for newborn check-ups, timely vaccinations, growth and nutrition advice, and quick, careful treatment when a child falls ill, whether it's a fever, a cough that won't settle, a skin rash or a tummy upset.

Children are not small adults. Every visit is unhurried and friendly for the child, and every parent leaves knowing what to watch for and what to do next.
