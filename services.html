01 / Newborn care

Newborn examination & care

The first weeks shape a baby's health. Dr. Patil checks breathing, vital signs, reflexes, weight and jaundice, and looks for any congenital conditions early. Parents get hands-on help with breastfeeding, cord care, hygiene and safe sleep.

We also advise on the recommended screening tests for metabolic, hearing and heart conditions, and start your baby's vaccinations on time.

  • Newborn examination
  • Jaundice monitoring
  • Breastfeeding support
  • Cord care
  • Safe sleep advice

03 / Growth & nutrition

Growth, development & nutrition

Regular check-ups track height, weight, head size and BMI on standard growth charts, along with motor, language and social milestones. This helps us catch delays, poor nutrition, hormonal or developmental concerns early, when they are easiest to treat.

Poor nutrition can slow growth, weaken immunity and cause anaemia. We create simple, age-appropriate diet plans, recommend vitamins where needed and follow up until your child is back on track.

  • Growth charts
  • Milestone checks
  • Diet counselling
  • Malnutrition care

Conditions we treat

When your child is unwell, we can help.

Skin problems

A baby's skin is thin and sensitive, so rashes and infections are common. Most settle quickly with the right care.

Diaper rashEczemaHeat rashCradle capFungal & bacterial infectionsScabiesRingwormHivesInsect bites

Childhood asthma

Swollen airways cause wheezing, cough and breathlessness. Inhalers, trigger control and our nebulization facility keep flare-ups in check.

WheezingInhaler trainingNebulization

Pneumonia

Young children's lungs are more vulnerable to infection. Fast breathing or low energy with fever needs prompt attention, and vaccines help prevent it.

Fast breathingFever with coughPrevention

Typhoid

Spread through unsafe food and water, typhoid causes a long-lasting fever, weakness and tummy pain. Early testing and treatment prevent complications.

Persistent feverVaccine at 9 months

Dengue & malaria

Watch for fever with chills, headache, pain behind the eyes, body aches, rashes or low platelets, and see a doctor right away.

DiagnosisPlatelet monitoringTreatment

Jaundice

Yellowing from high bilirubin is common in newborns but must be monitored closely. In older children it can follow contaminated food or water.

Newborn jaundiceBilirubin checks

Intestinal worms

Worms from soil, dirty hands or unclean food can cause tummy pain, poor appetite, anaemia and slow growth. Regular deworming and hygiene keep them away.

DewormingAnaemiaHygiene advice
