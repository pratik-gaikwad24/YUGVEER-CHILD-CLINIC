Meet Dr. Prashant Patil, M.D. (Paediatrics), the doctor behind Yugveer Child Clinic on Shiv Road, Moshi.

Our story

A clinic built around little ones.

Reg. No. I-94992-A

Yugveer Child Clinic opened in 2025 with one simple aim: to give the families of Moshi and Borhadewadi a paediatrician they can trust, close to home and open when they need one.

Dr. Prashant Patil holds an M.D. in Paediatrics and looks after children from their first day of life to their late teens. His experience with newborns, including care in the neonatal unit, means parents can bring even the tiniest babies with confidence.

The clinic covers everyday illness, vaccinations, growth and nutrition, and long-term conditions like asthma and allergies, so families don't have to run between different places for their child's care.

Growing up with us

One doctor through every milestone.

Day 0 – 28

Welcome to the world

Newborn examination, temperature and feeding support, jaundice and weight checks, cord care and first vaccines.

1 – 12 months

The busy first year

Vaccines at 1½, 2½ and 3½ months, flu shots at 6 and 7 months, milestone checks, and guidance for anxious new parents.

1 – 5 years

Toddler adventures

Booster doses, nutrition for picky eaters, and quick care for fevers, coughs and tummy bugs.

5 – 10 years

School days

School health certificates, allergy and asthma control, and regular growth assessments.

10 – 18 years

Teen years

Tdap / Td and HPV vaccines, growth and puberty concerns, and confidential adolescent health guidance.
